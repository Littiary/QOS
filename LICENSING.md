# QOS Licensing

QOS uses a dual license model.

## 1. Open source, AGPL-3.0

The whole repository (`qos-core`, `qos-runtime`, adapters, benchmarks) is
available under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. You can use, modify
and redistribute QOS freely as long as derivative works that you distribute or
run as a network service are also released under AGPL-3.0.

This keeps QOS open for students, researchers, hobbyists and open source
engines, and stops closed source products from absorbing it silently.

## 2. Commercial license

Studios and companies that want to embed QOS in closed source products (games,
engines, tools, SaaS) without AGPL obligations can get a commercial license.

Contact: Kaan Esrefoglu, Kestro Games. https://www.kestrogames.com

## Notes

- The canonical AGPL-3.0 text lives in `LICENSE`.
- Models (`.onnx`) produced by your own runs of `qos-core` with your own configs
  are your outputs. The license covers the QOS source code and shipped assets.
- The whitepaper in `paper/` is (c) Kaan Esrefoglu / Kestro Games, all rights
  reserved. It is documentation, not code.
